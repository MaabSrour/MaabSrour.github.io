Research Lab

Empowering Palestinian Voices

Objective

Detect and explain patterns that may restrict the visibility of Palestinian expression across major social platforms.

Dataset

Facebook 15,300 · Instagram 1,800 · X 12,600 · Total 29,700

Methods

TF-IDF, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, LinearSVC, SGD, mBERT, XLM-R, sentiment and emotion analysis.

Key results

Best accuracy reached 95.41% on X; Facebook ROC-AUC reached 0.97. The work also compared emotion and moderation patterns across platforms.
